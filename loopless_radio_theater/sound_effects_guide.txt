
🎵 RADIO THEATER SOUND EFFECTS GUIDE
===================================

REQUIRED SOUND EFFECTS:
You can find these on freesound.org or similar:

🏠 TAVERN ATMOSPHERE:
- Crackling fireplace (continuous background)
- Rain outside (gentle, atmospheric)
- Distant tavern murmur (very quiet)

🌊 OCEAN/BOAT SOUNDS:
- Gentle waves lapping
- Boat creaking (subtle)
- Seagull calls (distant)
- Water ambiance

⚡ SYSTEM CHAOS:
- Electronic glitching: bzzt-click-whirr-BUZZ
- Computer error sounds
- System startup/shutdown chimes
- Gentle electronic hums

🎵 MUSICAL ELEMENTS:
- Casey's seven impossible notes (mystical, beautiful)
- Transition music between scenes
- Closing theme music

📻 RADIO THEATER ASSEMBLY:
1. Import all generated MP3 segments
2. Layer background sounds underneath dialogue
3. Add sound effects at specified moments
4. Balance levels: dialogue prominent, effects supporting
5. Add music transitions between major scenes

RECOMMENDED FREE TOOLS:
- Audacity (free, cross-platform)
- GarageBand (Mac)
- Reaper (free trial, then $60)

Your radio theater will be 12-15 minutes of compelling audio! 📻
