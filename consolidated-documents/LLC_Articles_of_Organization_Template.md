## Filing Instructions:
1. Complete all bracketed fields
2. File with your state's Secretary of State office
3. Pay required filing fee (typically $50-$500 depending on state)
4. Obtain certified copies for banking and business purposes

## Required Documents to File Simultaneously:
- EIN Application (Form SS-4)
- Operating Agreement (even if single-member LLC)
- Initial Report (if required by your state)

## Next Steps After Filing:
1. Obtain EIN from IRS
2. Open business bank account
3. File beneficial ownership information (BOI) if required
4. Register for state/local taxes
5. Obtain necessary business licenses
6. File annual reports as required by state
