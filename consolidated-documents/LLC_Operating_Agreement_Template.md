## Article 3 - Management

### 3.1 Management Structure
The Company shall be managed by:
☐ All Members collectively
☐ Designated Manager(s): [MANAGER NAMES]

### 3.2 Authority of Managers/Members
The Manager(s)/Members have authority to:
- Conduct day-to-day operations
- Enter into contracts up to $[AMOUNT] without member approval
- Hire and terminate employees
- Make ordinary business decisions

### 3.3 Major Decisions Requiring Member Approval
The following require approval of Members holding [PERCENTAGE]% of membership interests:
- Amendments to this Agreement
- Admission of new Members
- Dissolution of the Company
- Sale of substantially all assets
- Taking on debt exceeding $[AMOUNT]
- Changing the Company's principal business

### 3.4 Meetings
- Annual meetings shall be held each [MONTH]
- Special meetings may be called by any Member
- [PERCENTAGE]% of membership interests constitute a quorum

## Article 4 - Financial Provisions

### 4.1 Capital Accounts
A capital account shall be maintained for each Member reflecting:
- Initial and subsequent capital contributions
- Share of Company profits and losses
- Distributions received

### 4.2 Allocation of Profits and Losses
Profits and losses shall be allocated among Members in proportion to their membership interests.

### 4.3 Distributions
Distributions shall be made:
- In proportion to membership interests
- When approved by [VOTING THRESHOLD]
- After setting aside reasonable reserves

## Article 5 - Books and Records

### 5.1 Records
The Company shall maintain:
- Books and records of account
- Federal and state tax returns
- This Operating Agreement and amendments
- Financial statements

### 5.2 Inspection Rights
Members may inspect Company records during normal business hours with reasonable notice.

## Article 6 - Dissolution

### 6.1 Events Triggering Dissolution
The Company shall dissolve upon:
- Vote of Members holding [PERCENTAGE]% of interests
- Death, withdrawal, or incapacity of sole Member (if applicable)
- Entry of judicial decree of dissolution

### 6.2 Winding Up Process
Upon dissolution:
1. Cease all business operations
2. Pay all debts and obligations
3. Distribute remaining assets to Members per their interests
