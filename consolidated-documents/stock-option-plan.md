# 2025 EMPLOYEE STOCK OPTION PLAN
## ACTIVELOG, INC.

**Effective Date:** [DATE]  
**Plan Administrator:** Board of Directors or Compensation Committee  
**Total Shares Reserved:** 1,000,000 shares of Class B Common Stock  

---

## ARTICLE I - ESTABLISHMENT AND PURPOSE

### 1.1 Establishment
ActiveLog, Inc. (the "Company") hereby establishes the "2025 Employee Stock Option Plan" (the "Plan") to attract, motivate, and retain qualified personnel.

### 1.2 Purpose
The purposes of this Plan are to:
- Attract and retain the best available personnel for positions of substantial responsibility
- Provide additional incentive to employees, directors, and consultants
- Promote the success of the Company's business by aligning employee interests with shareholders
- Enable employees to participate in the long-term growth and success of the Company

---

## ARTICLE II - DEFINITIONS

**"Board"** means the Board of Directors of the Company.

**"Committee"** means the Compensation Committee or such other committee designated by the Board to administer the Plan.

**"Common Stock"** means the Class B Common Stock of the Company, par value $0.001 per share.

**"Employee"** means any person employed by the Company or its subsidiaries.

**"Exercise Price"** means the price per share at which Common Stock may be purchased upon exercise of an Option.

**"Fair Market Value"** means the value determined in accordance with Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code.

**"ISO"** means an Incentive Stock Option that qualifies under Section 422 of the Internal Revenue Code.

**"NQSO"** means a Non-Qualified Stock Option that does not qualify as an ISO.

**"Option"** means a right to purchase Common Stock granted under this Plan.

**"Participant"** means an Employee, director, or consultant who has been granted an Option under this Plan.

---

## ARTICLE III - ADMINISTRATION

### 3.1 Committee Administration
The Plan shall be administered by the Committee, which shall have full authority to:
- Select participants and determine the terms of each Option grant
- Interpret the Plan and establish administrative rules
- Delegate administrative duties to officers or employees
- Hire advisors and delegate ministerial functions to third parties

### 3.2 Committee Composition
The Committee shall consist of at least two (2) directors who are:
- "Non-employee directors" under Rule 16b-3
- "Outside directors" under Section 162(m) of the Internal Revenue Code
- Independent under applicable stock exchange rules

### 3.3 Committee Decisions
All determinations by the Committee shall be final and binding on all parties.

---

## ARTICLE IV - SHARES SUBJECT TO PLAN

### 4.1 Share Reserve
The maximum number of shares of Common Stock that may be issued under the Plan is **1,000,000 shares**, subject to adjustment for stock splits, dividends, and other corporate events.

### 4.2 Share Counting
- Shares subject to expired or terminated Options shall become available for future grants
- Shares withheld to satisfy tax obligations shall count against the Plan limit
- Shares purchased with Option proceeds shall not be added back to the Plan

### 4.3 Annual Increase
On January 1 of each year, the number of shares available under the Plan shall automatically increase by the lesser of:
- 200,000 shares
- 5% of outstanding Common Stock on December 31 of the prior year
- Such lesser number as determined by the Board

---

## ARTICLE V - ELIGIBILITY AND PARTICIPATION

### 5.1 Eligible Participants
The following persons are eligible to receive Options under the Plan:
- All employees of the Company and its subsidiaries
- Non-employee directors of the Company
- Consultants providing services to the Company

### 5.2 Selection of Participants
The Committee shall select participants from eligible persons based on:
- Level of responsibility and potential contribution to Company success
- Individual performance and potential for future performance  
- Need to attract, retain, or motivate the individual
- Such other factors as the Committee deems relevant

---

## ARTICLE VI - STOCK OPTIONS

### 6.1 Grant of Options
The Committee may grant ISOs or NQSOs to eligible participants. Each Option shall be evidenced by a written agreement specifying:
- Number of shares subject to the Option
- Exercise price per share
- Vesting schedule and exercise periods
- Whether the Option is an ISO or NQSO
- Other terms and conditions

### 6.2 Exercise Price
The exercise price per share shall be determined by the Committee but shall not be less than:
- For ISOs: 100% of Fair Market Value on the grant date (110% for 10% shareholders)
- For NQSOs: 100% of Fair Market Value on the grant date

### 6.3 Vesting
Options shall vest according to the schedule set forth in the Option agreement, typically:
- 25% on the first anniversary of the vesting commencement date
- 75% in equal monthly installments over the following 36 months
- No vesting during the first 12 months (one-year cliff)

### 6.4 Exercise Period
- Options may be exercised only while vested and unexpired
- Standard exercise period is 10 years from grant date
- ISOs exercised more than 3 months after termination of employment become NQSOs
- Exercise period may be shortened upon termination of service

### 6.5 Method of Exercise
Options may be exercised by:
- Cash payment of full exercise price
- Cashless exercise through broker-dealer
- Net exercise using Option shares to pay exercise price
- Combination of above methods (if permitted by Committee)

---

## ARTICLE VII - INCENTIVE STOCK OPTIONS

### 7.1 ISO Requirements
ISOs must comply with Section 422 of the Internal Revenue Code:
- Only employees may receive ISOs
- Exercise price must equal Fair Market Value on grant date
- Maximum term is 10 years (5 years for 10% shareholders)
- $100,000 annual ISO limit (based on Fair Market Value at grant)

### 7.2 ISO Disqualifying Dispositions
If ISO shares are sold within 2 years of grant or 1 year of exercise:
- The sale becomes a disqualifying disposition
- Ordinary income treatment applies to the gain
- The Company may be entitled to a tax deduction

---

## ARTICLE VIII - TERMINATION OF SERVICE

### 8.1 Termination for Cause
Upon termination for Cause:
- All unvested Options are immediately forfeited
- All vested Options expire immediately
- No exercise is permitted after termination date

### 8.2 Voluntary Resignation
Upon voluntary resignation:
- All unvested Options are forfeited as of termination date
- Vested Options remain exercisable for 90 days after termination
- ISOs exercised more than 3 months after termination become NQSOs

### 8.3 Involuntary Termination
Upon involuntary termination without Cause:
- Unvested Options are forfeited (unless Committee provides for acceleration)
- Vested Options remain exercisable for 90 days
- Committee may provide for partial vesting acceleration

### 8.4 Death or Disability
Upon death or permanent disability:
- All Options become immediately vested and exercisable
- Options remain exercisable for 12 months
- Estate or guardian may exercise on behalf of Participant

### 8.5 Change in Control
Upon a Change in Control:
- Committee may accelerate vesting of all outstanding Options
- Options may be assumed, substituted, or cashed out
- Double-trigger acceleration may apply to key employees

---

## ARTICLE IX - TRANSFERABILITY

### 9.1 General Rule
Options are not transferable except by will or laws of descent and distribution, and may be exercised during the Participant's lifetime only by the Participant.

### 9.2 Permitted Transfers
The Committee may permit transfer of NQSOs to:
- Family members (spouse, children, grandchildren, parents)
- Trusts for the benefit of family members
- Family limited partnerships
- Other entities controlled by the Participant or family members

### 9.3 Transfer Restrictions
Any permitted transfer must:
- Be for no consideration
- Comply with applicable securities laws
- Include transferee agreement to be bound by Plan terms

---

## ARTICLE X - ADJUSTMENT PROVISIONS

### 10.1 Corporate Transactions
In the event of stock splits, stock dividends, recapitalizations, or similar transactions, the Committee shall adjust:
- Number of shares subject to outstanding Options
- Exercise price of outstanding Options
- Number of shares available under the Plan

### 10.2 Merger or Sale
In the event of a merger, sale of assets, or similar transaction:
- Options may be assumed by the surviving entity
- Options may be substituted with equivalent rights
- Options may be cashed out at fair value
- Vesting may be accelerated (single or double trigger)

### 10.3 Dissolution
If the Company dissolves:
- All Options terminate immediately prior to dissolution
- Participants have the right to exercise vested Options before dissolution
- Committee may accelerate vesting of unvested Options

---

## ARTICLE XI - GENERAL PROVISIONS

### 11.1 Securities Law Compliance
No Option shall be exercisable unless:
- The shares are registered under applicable securities laws, or
- The exercise qualifies for an exemption from registration
- Such other conditions as required by law are satisfied

### 11.2 Tax Withholding
The Company may withhold taxes by:
- Requiring cash payment of withholding taxes
- Withholding shares from Option exercise
- Deducting from other compensation
- Allowing sale of shares to cover taxes

### 11.3 No Right to Employment
Participation in the Plan does not give any person the right to:
- Continue employment with the Company
- Any claim against the Company for damages arising from termination
- Participate in any other benefit plan

### 11.4 Governing Law
This Plan shall be governed by Delaware state law, except as superseded by federal law.

### 11.5 Amendment and Termination
The Board may amend or terminate this Plan at any time, subject to:
- Shareholder approval for certain amendments
- No impairment of existing Option rights without consent
- Compliance with applicable legal requirements

## SCHEDULE A - STANDARD VESTING SCHEDULE

**New Hire Grants:**
- Vesting Commencement: Start date or grant date
- Cliff: 12 months (25% vests on first anniversary)
- Monthly Vesting: 1/48th of total grant each month for months 13-48
- Full Vesting: 48 months after vesting commencement date

**Promotion/Performance Grants:**
- Vesting Commencement: Grant date
- Cliff: 6 months (12.5% vests on first semi-annual anniversary)
- Quarterly Vesting: 1/16th of total grant each quarter
- Full Vesting: 48 months after grant date
