## Delaware-Specific Provisions (if incorporating in Delaware):

### Article XI - Delaware Forum Selection
Unless the corporation consents in writing to the selection of an alternative forum, the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware shall be the sole and exclusive forum for:
- Derivative actions brought on behalf of the corporation
- Actions asserting breach of fiduciary duty claims
- Actions asserting claims under the Delaware General Corporation Law

### Article XII - Federal Forum Selection
Unless the corporation consents in writing to the selection of an alternative forum, the federal district courts of the United States shall be the exclusive forum for actions asserting claims under the Securities Act of 1933.
