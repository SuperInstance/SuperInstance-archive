# Business Insurance Requirements Guide

## Essential Business Insurance Types

### 1. General Liability Insurance
**Coverage:** Protects against third-party claims of bodily injury, property damage, and personal/advertising injury
- **Typical Cost:** $400-$1,500 annually
- **Coverage Limits:** $1M per occurrence, $2M aggregate
- **Required For:** All businesses with customer/vendor interaction

### 2. Professional Liability (E&O) Insurance
**Coverage:** Protects against claims of professional negligence, errors, and omissions
- **Typical Cost:** $500-$3,000 annually
- **Coverage Limits:** $1M-$5M per claim
- **Required For:** Service-based businesses, consultants, tech companies

### 3. Cyber Liability Insurance
**Coverage:** Data breach response, cyber attacks, privacy violations
- **Typical Cost:** $500-$2,500 annually
- **Coverage Limits:** $1M-$5M
- **Required For:** Any business handling customer data

### 4. Directors & Officers (D&O) Insurance
**Coverage:** Protects executives from personal liability for company decisions
- **Typical Cost:** $1,000-$5,000 annually
- **Required For:** Corporations with outside investors

### 5. Employment Practices Liability (EPL)
**Coverage:** Discrimination, harassment, wrongful termination claims
- **Typical Cost:** $800-$3,000 annually
- **Required For:** Companies with employees

## Industry-Specific Requirements

### Technology Companies:
- Professional Liability: $2M-$10M
- Cyber Liability: $5M-$50M
- Technology E&O: Covers software defects
- Patent Defense: IP infringement claims

### Healthcare/Biotech:
- Medical Malpractice: $1M-$10M per claim
- Clinical Trial Insurance: Research-specific coverage
- HIPAA Compliance Coverage
- Product Liability for medical devices

### Financial Services:
- Fidelity Bond: Employee dishonesty
- Crime Insurance: Theft and fraud
- Fiduciary Liability: Investment advice
- Regulatory Defense: Compliance violations

## Required vs. Optional Coverage

### Legally Required:
- Workers' Compensation (if you have employees)
- Unemployment Insurance (state-specific)
- Commercial Auto (if company vehicles)

### Contractually Required:
- General Liability (often required by leases/contracts)
- Professional Liability (client contracts)
- Cyber Liability (vendor agreements)

### Recommended Optional:
- Business Interruption Insurance
- Key Person Life Insurance
- Commercial Property Insurance

## Coverage Amounts by Company Stage

### Startup (Pre-Revenue):
- General Liability: $1M/$2M
- Professional Liability: $1M
- Cyber Liability: $1M
- D&O: $1M

### Growth Stage ($1M+ Revenue):
- General Liability: $2M/$4M
- Professional Liability: $2M-$5M
- Cyber Liability: $5M-$10M
- D&O: $5M-$10M

### Enterprise (Series B+):
- General Liability: $5M+
- Professional Liability: $10M+
- Cyber Liability: $25M+
- D&O: $25M+

## Getting Insurance Quotes

### Information You'll Need:
- Business description and industry
- Revenue and employee count
- Location and facilities
- Previous claims history
- Security measures implemented

### Best Practices:
- Get quotes from 3+ carriers
- Work with an experienced broker
- Review policy exclusions carefully
- Consider package policies for cost savings

## Annual Insurance Review Checklist

- [ ] Review coverage limits vs. current business size
- [ ] Update business description if changed
- [ ] Review new regulatory requirements
- [ ] Compare quotes for potential savings
- [ ] Update certificate holders
- [ ] Review claims history impact
