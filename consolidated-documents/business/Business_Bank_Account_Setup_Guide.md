# Business Bank Account Setup Guide

## Required Documents Checklist

### For LLCs:
- [ ] Articles of Organization (certified copy)
- [ ] Operating Agreement
- [ ] EIN Letter from IRS (SS-4 or CP-575)
- [ ] Government-issued ID for all authorized signers
- [ ] Proof of business address

### For Corporations:
- [ ] Articles of Incorporation (certified copy)
- [ ] Corporate Bylaws
- [ ] Board Resolution authorizing bank account
- [ ] EIN Letter from IRS
- [ ] Government-issued ID for all authorized signers
- [ ] Stock certificates or capitalization table

### Additional Documents (if applicable):
- [ ] DBA/Fictitious Name Certificate
- [ ] Business License
- [ ] Partnership Agreement (for partnerships)
- [ ] Trust Agreement (for trusts)

## Choosing the Right Bank

### Business-Friendly Banks:
**Large National Banks:**
- Chase Business: Good for high transaction volume
- Bank of America: Comprehensive services
- Wells Fargo: Strong business lending

**Regional/Community Banks:**
- Better customer service
- More flexible lending criteria
- Local decision making

**Online Banks:**
- Azlo (discontinued - acquired by BBVA)
- Novo: Tech-focused features
- BlueVine: Integrated lending
- Mercury: Designed for startups

### Account Types:

**Business Checking:**
- Monthly fees: $10-$30
- Transaction limits: 100-500/month
- Minimum balance: $100-$2,500

**Business Savings:**
- Interest rates: 0.01%-2.00%
- Monthly fees: $5-$15
- Minimum balance: $300-$10,000

**Money Market:**
- Higher interest rates
- Limited transactions
- Higher minimum balances

## Fee Structures to Compare

### Monthly Maintenance Fees:
- Range: $10-$30
- Often waived with minimum balance
- Consider transaction volume

### Transaction Fees:
- Deposits: Usually free for first 100-500
- Checks: $0.15-$0.50 each after allowance
- Wire transfers: $15-$30 domestic, $35-$50 international
- ACH transfers: $0.10-$1.00 each

### Other Fees:
- Overdraft: $35 per occurrence
- Cash deposits: May charge for large amounts
- Check printing: $20-$50 per order
- Account research: $25-$50 per hour

## Special Considerations for Startups

### Mercury Bank (Popular for Tech Startups):
- No monthly fees
- No minimum balance
- Free ACH/wire transfers
- Integrated with startup tools
- FDIC insured
- Online/mobile only

### SVB (Silicon Valley Bank) - Note: Check current status:

- International capabilities
- Higher minimum balances required

### First Republic (Check current availability):
- Premium service model
- Relationship-based banking
- Attractive rates for deposits

## Account Setup Process

### Step 1: Gather Documents
Complete the required documents checklist above.

### Step 2: Schedule Appointment
- Call or schedule online
- Some banks allow online applications
- Plan for 30-60 minutes

### Step 3: Initial Deposit
- Minimum: $25-$100
- Bring cashier's check or cash
- Personal checks may have holds

### Step 4: Order Checks and Debit Cards
- Business checks: 2-3 weeks delivery
- Debit cards: 7-10 business days
- Consider expedited shipping

### Step 5: Set Up Online Banking
- Enable online access immediately
- Set up mobile app
- Configure account alerts

## Banking Best Practices

### Separation of Business and Personal:
- Never mix personal and business funds
- Maintain separate accounts from day one
- Keep detailed records of all transactions

### Account Management:
- Reconcile accounts monthly
- Set up account alerts for low balances
- Review statements for unauthorized transactions
- Keep business records for 7 years

### Cash Flow Management:
- Maintain 3-6 months operating expenses
- Set up automatic transfers to savings
- Consider business credit line for cash flow

## Additional Financial Services to Consider

### Business Credit Cards:
- Helps build business credit history
- Separates business expenses
- Often includes rewards programs
- Apply after establishing checking account

### Business Lines of Credit:
- Flexible funding for cash flow
- Only pay interest on amounts used
- Typically require 6-12 months banking history

### Merchant Services:
- Accept credit card payments
- Often bundled with bank accounts
- Compare rates: 1.5%-3.5% per transaction

### Payroll Services:
- Automated payroll processing
- Tax filing and payments
- Direct deposit for employees

## International Considerations

### Multi-Currency Accounts:
- Hold funds in multiple currencies
- Reduce foreign exchange costs
- Good for international businesses

### Wire Transfer Capabilities:
- International wire transfers
- Correspondent banking relationships
- SWIFT codes and routing information

### Foreign Exchange Services:
- Competitive FX rates
- Forward contracts for rate protection
- Regular international payments

## Technology Integration

### Accounting Software Integration:
- QuickBooks connectivity
- Automatic transaction import
- Reconciliation features

### Payment Processing:
- Stripe/PayPal integration
- ACH payment processing
- Recurring billing capabilities

### API Access:
- For custom integrations
- Real-time balance checking
- Transaction automation

## Red Flags to Avoid

### Bank Selection:
- Excessive fees with no waivers
- Poor online banking platform
- Limited business services
- No local branches (if needed)

### Account Management:
- Mixing personal and business funds
- Inadequate record keeping
- Ignoring account terms and conditions
- Not monitoring for fraud
