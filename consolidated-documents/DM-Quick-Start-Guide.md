# DMLog Dungeon Master Quick-Start Guide

*Welcome to DMLog - Your Complete D&D Campaign Management System*

## 🎲 Getting Started in 5 Minutes

### Step 1: Launch Your First Campaign
1. **Create a New Campaign**
   - Click "Start New Campaign" on the dashboard
   - Choose from our pre-made templates or create custom
   - **Recommended**: Start with "The Goblin Caves" (Level 1-3)

2. **Invite Your Players**
   - Share the campaign code with your players
   - They can join via the web interface or mobile app
   - Real-time synchronization keeps everyone connected

### Step 2: Set Up Characters (5 minutes)
**For New Players:**
- Use our character templates:
  - Human Fighter (beginner-friendly)
  - Elf Wizard (magic user)  
  - Halfling Rogue (skill-focused)
  - Dwarf Cleric (healer/support)
  - Half-Orc Barbarian (damage dealer)

**For Experienced Players:**
- Full character creation with all D&D 5e options
- Import from D&D Beyond, Roll20, or other platforms
- Custom character art upload support

### Step 3: Launch Your Session
1. **Start the Session Manager**
   - All players see real-time updates
   - Shared initiative tracker
   - Group dice rolling visible to all

2. **Begin with "The Goblin Caves"**
   - Pre-loaded maps and encounters
   - NPCs with full personality profiles
   - Automated treasure and XP distribution

## 🎯 Running Your First Session

### Pre-Session (10 minutes)
1. **Review the Adventure**
   - Read "The Goblin Caves" summary
   - Check NPC notes and motivations
   - Familiarize yourself with the map

2. **Prepare Materials**
   - Load the battle maps in DMLog
   - Queue up ambient forest sounds
   - Have character tokens ready

3. **Set the Scene**
   - Brief players on the village of Thornwick
   - Establish character relationships
   - Review basic game rules if needed

### During the Session

#### Opening Scene: "The Wounded Caravan"
- **Read-aloud text provided in the adventure**
- Use the **Dice Roller** for Medicine and Insight checks
- **Track Clues** as players gather information

#### Combat Encounters
1. **Forest Ambush**:
   - Load the forest battle map
   - Place goblin tokens using the map editor
   - Use **Initiative Tracker** - DMLog handles the math!
   - **Sound Effects**: Enable combat music and effects

2. **Cave Encounters**:
   - Switch to cave maps automatically
   - **Dynamic Lighting**: Reveal areas as they explore
   - **Trap Detection**: Built-in DC tracking

#### Roleplay Scenes
- **NPC Voices**: Use the personality notes for unique voices
- **Social Encounters**: Built-in DC suggestions for persuasion/intimidation
- **Real-time Notes**: Players can see your world-building notes

### Ending the Session
1. **XP Distribution**: DMLog calculates automatically
2. **Loot Distribution**: Drag items to character sheets
3. **Session Recap**: Auto-generated summary of key events
4. **Next Session Preview**: Tease upcoming content

## 🎭 Roleplaying Tips

### NPC Voices and Mannerisms
- **Goblins**: High-pitched, broken Common, aggressive gestures
- **Village Elder**: Slow, thoughtful speech, worried expressions
- **Merchants**: Fast-talking, emphasize monetary concerns
- **Guards**: Formal speech, military bearing

### Pacing Your Session
- **25% Combat**: Action and excitement
- **25% Exploration**: Discovery and problem-solving
- **25% Roleplay**: Character development and story
- **25% Downtime**: Planning and character interaction

### Managing Player Expectations
- **Session Zero**: Discuss game style and boundaries
- **Regular Check-ins**: Ask if players are having fun
- **Flexible Storytelling**: Adapt to player choices
- **Celebrate Success**: Acknowledge good roleplay and clever solutions

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### Technical Issues
- **Connection Problems**: DMLog works offline with sync when reconnected
- **Browser Performance**: Chrome/Firefox recommended, disable other tabs
- **Mobile Sync Issues**: Force refresh the companion app

### Game Flow Issues
- **Players Distracted**: Use timers for decisions
- **Combat Too Slow**: Pre-roll damage, use average damage for minions
- **Players Lost**: Provide clear objectives and recap regularly

### Common New DM Mistakes
- **Over-Planning**: Be ready to improvise
- **Too Many Rules**: Focus on fun over perfect rule adherence
- **Saying "No" Too Much**: Look for ways to say "Yes, but..."
- **Not Taking Breaks**: 10-minute breaks every 2 hours
