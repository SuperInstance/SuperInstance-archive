## OFFERING TERMS COMPLIANCE ✓

### 1. Investment Limits (Section 227.100)
- [ ] **Individual Investor Limits Programmed**
  - Income/net worth < $107K: Lesser of $2,200 or 5% of income/net worth
  - Income/net worth ≥ $107K: 10% of income/net worth, max $107K
  - System validation of limits implemented

- [ ] **Aggregate Offering Limit**
  - Maximum raise: $5,000,000 (12-month period)
  - Current raise amount: $5,000,000
  - Tracking system for 12-month rolling period

### 2. Offering Period (Section 227.200)
- [ ] **Timing Requirements**
  - Minimum offering period: 21 days
  - Maximum offering period: 120 days
  - Cancellation rights: 48 hours before deadline
  - Material change notification: 5-day re-confirmation

### 3. Intermediary Requirements (Section 227.300)
- [ ] **Funding Portal Selection**
  - SEC-registered funding portal selected
  - FINRA member verification
  - Portal agreement executed
  - Escrow account arrangements confirmed
