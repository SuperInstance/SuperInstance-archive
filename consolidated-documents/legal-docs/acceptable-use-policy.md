# ActiveLog Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective Date:** [DATE]  
**Last Updated:** [DATE]

## 1. Introduction and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose
This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs your use of ActiveLog's services, platforms, and resources. It is designed to protect ActiveLog, our users, and the broader internet community from activities that could harm our services, compromise security, or violate applicable laws.

### 1.2 Scope
This AUP applies to all ActiveLog services, including:
- ActiveLog main platform and development environment
- ActiveLog Kids educational platform
- API services and integrations
- Community forums and collaboration features
- Customer support and communication channels
- Any other services provided by ActiveLog Inc.

### 1.3 Binding Agreement
By using ActiveLog services, you agree to comply with this AUP. Violations may result in service suspension, account termination, or legal action.

## 2. General Principles

### 2.1 Lawful Use
You must use ActiveLog services in compliance with:
- All applicable federal, state, local, and international laws
- Industry regulations and standards
- Professional codes of conduct
- ActiveLog's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy

### 2.2 Respectful Community
ActiveLog fosters a respectful, inclusive community. Users must:
- Treat all community members with respect and dignity
- Respect diverse backgrounds, opinions, and perspectives
- Contribute constructively to discussions and collaborations
- Report inappropriate behavior through proper channels

### 2.3 Security and Integrity
Users must help maintain the security and integrity of ActiveLog services by:
- Protecting account credentials and access tokens
- Reporting security vulnerabilities responsibly
- Following secure coding and development practices
- Respecting system resources and performance

## 3. Prohibited Activities

### 3.1 Illegal Activities
You may not use ActiveLog services for:

**Criminal Activity:**
- Planning, facilitating, or engaging in any criminal activity
- Money laundering or financial fraud
- Identity theft or impersonation
- Cyberstalking or harassment
- Distribution of illegal substances
- Human trafficking or exploitation

**Intellectual Property Violations:**
- Copyright infringement or piracy
- Trademark violations
- Trade secret theft
- Patent infringement
- Unauthorized use of proprietary software or content

**Privacy Violations:**
- Unauthorized collection of personal information
- Doxxing or publishing private information without consent
- Surveillance or monitoring without authorization
- Identity theft or impersonation

### 3.2 Harmful Content
You may not upload, share, or distribute:

**Violent Content:**
- Content promoting or inciting violence
- Terrorist recruitment or propaganda
- Instructions for creating weapons or explosives
- Graphic violence or gore
- Content promoting self-harm or suicide

**Hateful Content:**
- Content promoting hatred based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, or other protected characteristics
- Nazi or white supremacist materials
- Content promoting discrimination or harassment
- Extremist ideologies or propaganda

**Adult Content:**
- Sexually explicit materials (except in specifically designated areas)
- Child exploitation materials (strictly prohibited)
- Non-consensual intimate imagery
- Adult services or escort advertisements

**Harmful to Minors:**
- Content inappropriate for children on ActiveLog Kids
- Predatory behavior targeting minors
- Grooming or exploitation attempts
- Content that could harm child development

### 3.3 Spam and Abuse
You may not engage in:

**Spam Activities:**
- Mass distribution of unsolicited messages
- Advertising or promoting unrelated products/services
- Creating fake or duplicate accounts
- Automated posting or content generation for spam purposes
- Search engine optimization (SEO) spam

**System Abuse:**
- Attempting to gain unauthorized access to systems
- Interfering with or disrupting services
- Excessive consumption of system resources
- Circumventing security measures or rate limits
- Reverse engineering or decompiling software

### 3.4 Fraudulent Activities
You may not engage in:
- Creating fake accounts or impersonating others
- Providing false or misleading information
- Deceptive practices to gain unauthorized access
- Financial fraud or payment disputes without cause
- Creating misleading or deceptive content

## 4. Technical Violations

### 4.1 Security Violations
Prohibited security-related activities include:

**Unauthorized Access:**
- Attempting to access accounts or systems without permission
- Password cracking or brute force attacks
- Social engineering to obtain credentials
- Exploiting security vulnerabilities for malicious purposes

**Network Attacks:**
- Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks
- Network scanning or port scanning
- Packet sniffing or network interception
- Botnet creation or participation

**Malware and Exploits:**
- Distributing viruses, worms, or other malware
- Creating or distributing exploit code
- Keyloggers or other surveillance software
- Cryptocurrency mining without explicit permission

### 4.2 Platform Abuse
You may not:
- Create multiple accounts to evade restrictions
- Manipulate voting, rating, or feedback systems
- Scrape or harvest data without permission
- Use automated tools to abuse service features
- Attempt to reverse engineer proprietary algorithms

### 4.3 Resource Abuse
Prohibited resource usage includes:
- Excessive bandwidth consumption
- Running computationally intensive processes without authorization
- Storing or distributing large files unrelated to legitimate use
- Using services for cryptocurrency mining (unless explicitly permitted)
- Monopolizing shared resources to the detriment of other users

## 5. Content Guidelines

### 5.1 Code and Development Content
When sharing code and development content:

**Quality Standards:**
- Code should be functional and well-documented
- Avoid sharing intentionally malicious code
- Include appropriate licenses and attribution
- Follow community coding standards and best practices

**Security Considerations:**
- Do not include hardcoded passwords, API keys, or secrets
- Avoid sharing code with known security vulnerabilities
- Report security issues through proper channels
- Use secure coding practices in shared examples

### 5.2 Educational Content
For educational materials and tutorials:
- Ensure accuracy and educational value
- Provide proper attribution for third-party content
- Use age-appropriate language and examples
- Avoid misleading or outdated information
- Respect intellectual property rights

### 5.3 Community Contributions
When participating in forums, comments, or discussions:
- Stay on topic and contribute meaningfully
- Use professional and respectful language
- Cite sources for factual claims
- Respect others' opinions and experiences
- Help maintain a positive community environment

## 9. Reporting and Enforcement

### 9.1 Reporting Violations
To report AUP violations:

**Online Reporting:**
- Use the built-in reporting features
- Submit reports through our support portal
- Email abuse@activelog.com for serious violations
- Use emergency contacts for urgent safety issues

**Required Information:**
- Detailed description of the violation
- URLs, usernames, or other identifying information
- Screenshots or evidence where appropriate
- Your contact information for follow-up

### 9.2 Investigation Process
When violations are reported:
1. **Initial Review (24 hours):** Assess severity and take immediate action if needed
2. **Investigation (3-5 business days):** Gather evidence and review circumstances
3. **Decision (1-2 business days):** Determine appropriate response
4. **Notification:** Inform involved parties of decisions and actions
5. **Appeal Process:** Provide opportunity for appeal where appropriate

### 9.3 Enforcement Actions
Depending on violation severity, we may:

**Warning and Education:**
- Send warning email with policy explanation
- Require completion of educational modules
- Provide resources for compliance
- Monitor account for future violations

**Account Restrictions:**
- Limit access to certain features
- Impose usage quotas or rate limits
- Require additional verification
- Suspend specific privileges

**Temporary Suspension:**
- Suspend account access for specified period
- Remove violating content
- Require acknowledgment of violations
- Mandate compliance measures for reinstatement

**Permanent Termination:**
- Close account permanently
- Remove all associated content
- Ban from creating new accounts
- Forfeit paid subscriptions without refund

**Legal Action:**
- Report to law enforcement agencies
- Pursue civil litigation for damages
- Cooperate with regulatory investigations
- Seek injunctive relief where appropriate
