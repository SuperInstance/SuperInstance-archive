## 1. Introduction for Parents and Guardians

ActiveLog Inc. ("ActiveLog," "we," "our," or "us") takes children's privacy very seriously. This Children's Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information from children under 13 years of age who use ActiveLog Kids, our educational coding platform designed specifically for young learners.

**This policy is written for parents and guardians.** We encourage you to read this policy carefully and discuss it with your child to help them understand how we protect their privacy online.

### 1.1 COPPA Compliance Commitment

ActiveLog Kids is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and FTC regulations. We:
- Obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from children
- Collect only information necessary to provide our educational service
- Give parents access to their child's personal information
- Provide parents the opportunity to prevent further use or collection
- Maintain the confidentiality, security, and integrity of children's personal information

## 2. Information Collection from Children

### 2.1 Information We Collect with Parental Consent

**Account Information:**
- Child's username (not real name)
- Child's age or birth month/year for age verification
- Parent's email address for account management and communication
- Educational level or grade (optional)

**Educational Progress:**
- Completed lessons and achievements
- Coding projects and creations (stored privately)
- Learning progress and skill development
- Time spent using the platform (for healthy usage monitoring)

**Technical Information (Automatic Collection):**
- Device type and browser information (for technical support)
- General location information (country/state level only, for content localization)
- Usage patterns (for safety monitoring and service improvement)
- Error logs and performance data (for technical improvements)

### 2.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect the following information from children:
- Real names, home addresses, or telephone numbers
- Social security numbers or government identification
- Photos, videos, or audio recordings of the child
- Precise geolocation information
- Personal information through contests, surveys, or games without additional consent
- Information for behavioral advertising purposes
- Persistent identifiers for tracking across websites

### 2.3 Passive Collection Limitations

When information is automatically collected:
- We use only session cookies, not persistent tracking cookies
- We do not create personal profiles for advertising
- We do not track children across other websites
- We limit data collection to what's necessary for service provision

## 3. How We Use Children's Information

### 3.1 Permitted Uses

We use children's personal information only for:

**Educational Service Provision:**
- Creating and maintaining the child's account
- Providing coding lessons and educational content
- Tracking educational progress and achievements
- Customizing learning experiences appropriate to the child's level

**Safety and Security:**
- Monitoring for inappropriate behavior or content
- Protecting children from harmful interactions
- Maintaining platform security and preventing unauthorized access
- Complying with legal obligations and safety requirements

**Parent Communication:**
- Sending parents updates about their child's progress
- Notifying parents of any safety concerns
- Providing customer support to parents
- Sharing important service updates and policy changes

**Service Improvement:**
- Improving educational content and features (using aggregated, de-identified data)
- Fixing technical issues and bugs
- Enhancing safety measures and content moderation
- Developing age-appropriate new features

### 3.2 Prohibited Uses

We do not use children's information for:
- Behavioral advertising or targeted marketing
- Creating detailed personal profiles beyond educational purposes
- Selling or renting to third parties
- Social networking with unknown individuals
- Sharing with other users without parental consent

## 4. Parental Consent Process

### 4.1 Verifiable Parental Consent Methods

We obtain consent through FTC-approved methods:

**Credit Card Verification:**
- Small authorization charge (immediately refunded) to verify cardholder is an adult
- No payment information is stored after verification

**Signed Consent Form:**
- Digital signature on consent form
- Mailed or faxed signed consent form
- Government-issued photo ID verification

**Video Conference Verification:**
- Scheduled video call with parent or guardian
- Identity verification through photo ID
- Verbal consent recorded (with permission)

**Phone Verification Plus:**
- Phone call to verify identity
- Follow-up with additional verification method
- Documented consent process

### 4.2 Consent Scope

Parental consent covers:
- Collection of information described in this policy
- Use of information for educational purposes
- Internal use for service improvement (aggregated data only)
- Communication with parents about the child's account

**Additional consent is required for:**
- Sharing child's work publicly (even if anonymous)
- Participating in special programs or contests
- Integration with third-party educational tools
- Any material changes to our privacy practices

## 5. Parental Access and Control Rights

### 5.1 Review Rights

Parents can review:
- All personal information collected from their child
- Their child's account settings and preferences
- Educational progress and achievements
- Communication logs and interactions
- Privacy settings and data sharing permissions

### 5.2 Control Rights

Parents can:
- Update or correct their child's information
- Delete any or all of their child's personal information
- Refuse further collection of their child's information
- Request we stop using their child's information
- Control who can interact with their child
- Set usage time limits and restrictions

### 5.3 Access Methods

Parents can exercise these rights through:
- Secure parent dashboard (available 24/7)
- Email request to childprivacy@activelog.com
- Phone call to our Family Support line
- Written request sent to our mailing address

**Response Time:** We respond to parent requests within 24 hours for urgent matters and within 5 business days for all other requests.

## 6. Information Sharing and Disclosure

### 6.1 General Policy

We do not share, sell, or disclose children's personal information except as described in this policy and with appropriate parental consent or legal authorization.

### 6.2 Limited Sharing Circumstances

**Service Providers:**
- Cloud hosting providers (with strict data protection agreements)
- Technical support and security services
- Email services for parent communication
- All service providers are contractually required to protect children's information

**Legal Requirements:**
- Valid legal process (court orders, subpoenas)
- Child safety emergencies
- Compliance with law enforcement investigations
- Protection of our rights and property

**Educational Partners:**
- Schools and teachers (only with school account and parental consent)
- Educational content providers (aggregated data only)
- Curriculum developers (de-identified data only)

### 6.3 Prohibited Sharing

We never share children's information with:
- Advertisers or marketing companies
- Social media platforms
- Data brokers or analytics companies (for commercial purposes)
- Other users without explicit parental consent
- Any party for commercial gain

## 7. Data Security for Children

### 7.1 Enhanced Security Measures

**Technical Safeguards:**
- Military-grade encryption (AES-256) for all stored data
- Encrypted transmission (TLS 1.3) for all communications
- Multi-factor authentication for parent accounts
- Regular security audits and penetration testing
- Immediate detection and response to security incidents

**Access Controls:**
- Strict employee access controls with background checks
- Role-based access with minimum necessary permissions
- Regular access reviews and updates
- Mandatory privacy and security training for all staff
- Detailed audit logs of all data access

**Infrastructure Protection:**
- SOC 2 Type II certified data centers
- 24/7 security monitoring and threat detection
- DDoS protection and network security
- Regular backups and disaster recovery testing
- Physical security measures at all facilities

### 7.2 Breach Response Protocol

In the unlikely event of a security incident involving children's data:
1. **Immediate Response (0-4 hours):** Contain and assess the incident
2. **Parent Notification (within 24 hours):** Direct contact with affected parents
3. **Regulatory Notification (within 72 hours):** Report to FTC and other authorities
4. **Remediation (ongoing):** Implement additional protections and monitoring
5. **Follow-up (30 days):** Provide detailed incident report to parents

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

### 8.1 Retention Policy for Children

**Account Information:**
- Retained only while account is active and service is being provided
- Deleted within 30 days of account closure
- No retention for marketing or commercial purposes

**Educational Content:**
- Child's projects and creations deleted immediately upon parent request
- Learning progress data deleted when account is closed
- Aggregated, anonymous data may be retained for educational research

**Communication Records:**
- Parent-child communication deleted when account is closed
- Support communications with parents retained for 1 year maximum
- Safety incident records retained as required by law

### 8.2 Automatic Deletion

**Inactive Accounts:**
- Accounts inactive for 12 months are automatically flagged
- Parents notified before any deletion occurs
- Data deleted 30 days after parent notification if no response

**Age Transition:**
- When child turns 13, parents can choose to:
  - Transition to teen account with updated privacy settings
  - Delete all information and close account
  - Export child's projects before transition/deletion

### 8.3 Deletion Process

When information is deleted:
1. All copies in production systems are immediately removed
2. Backup systems are purged within 30 days
3. Partners and service providers confirm deletion
4. Parents receive written confirmation of complete deletion
