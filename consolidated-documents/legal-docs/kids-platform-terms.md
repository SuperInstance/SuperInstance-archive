## 1. Introduction - For Parents and Guardians

ActiveLog Kids is a special version of our platform designed specifically for children under 13 years of age. This service is provided by ActiveLog Inc. ("ActiveLog," "we," "our," or "us") with enhanced privacy protections and safety features in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and other applicable laws.

**IMPORTANT FOR PARENTS:** Before your child can use ActiveLog Kids, you must read and agree to these Terms as the parent or legal guardian. By creating an account for your child, you acknowledge that you have reviewed these Terms and our Children's Privacy Policy.

## 2. Parental Consent and Account Creation

### 2.1 Required Parental Consent
- A parent or legal guardian must provide verifiable consent before a child under 13 can create an account
- Consent is obtained through our verified parental consent process
- Parents maintain the right to review, modify, or delete their child's account at any time

### 2.2 Verification Process
We verify parental consent through:
- Credit card verification (small authorization charge that is immediately refunded)
- Signed consent form sent via mail or fax
- Video conference verification
- Other FTC-approved methods

### 2.3 Account Management
Parents have full control over their child's account and can:
- Review all account information and content
- Modify privacy settings and permissions
- Delete the account at any time
- Control who can interact with their child
- Set usage time limits and restrictions

## 3. Special Features for Children's Safety

### 3.1 Content Filtering
ActiveLog Kids includes enhanced content filtering:
- All user-generated content is pre-moderated
- Inappropriate language and content are automatically blocked
- Educational content is prioritized and curated
- External links are restricted or filtered through safe browsing

### 3.2 Communication Restrictions
To protect children's privacy and safety:
- Direct messaging between users is disabled by default
- All communications are monitored and filtered
- Personal information sharing is blocked
- Contact information cannot be exchanged
- Comments and forum posts are pre-moderated

### 3.3 Educational Focus
ActiveLog Kids emphasizes:
- Age-appropriate coding tutorials and exercises
- STEM education and creative problem-solving
- Safe collaboration on educational projects
- Positive reinforcement and achievement recognition
- Screen time awareness and healthy usage habits

## 4. Information Collection and Use (Child-Specific)

### 4.1 Limited Data Collection
We collect only information necessary to provide the service:
- Username (not the child's real name)
- Parent's email address for account management
- Age verification (birth month/year only)
- Educational progress and achievements
- Usage patterns for safety and improvement

### 4.2 Prohibited Data Collection
We DO NOT collect from children:
- Full names, addresses, or phone numbers
- Photos or videos containing the child's likeness
- Audio recordings of the child's voice
- Location information
- Social security numbers or government IDs
- Personal information through contests or surveys without additional consent

### 4.3 Data Use Restrictions
Information collected from children is used only to:
- Provide the educational service
- Ensure safety and security
- Communicate with parents about the child's account
- Comply with legal obligations
- Improve the service (using aggregated, de-identified data only)
