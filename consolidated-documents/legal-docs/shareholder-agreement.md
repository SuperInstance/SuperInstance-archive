## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

### 1.1 Definitions

**"Affiliate"** means any person or entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party.

**"Board"** means the Board of Directors of the Company.

**"Company"** means ActiveLog, Inc., a Delaware corporation.

**"Founder"** means any person listed as a founder in Schedule B, holding Class A Common Stock.

**"Major Investor"** means any shareholder holding at least 5% of the Company's outstanding shares.

**"Option Pool"** means shares of Class B Common Stock reserved for employee stock options and incentive plans.

**"Preferred Shares"** means any series of preferred stock issued by the Company.

**"Tag-Along Sale"** means any proposed transfer of shares by a Major Shareholder to a third party.

**"Transfer"** includes any sale, assignment, gift, pledge, hypothecation, or other disposition of shares.

### 1.2 Share Classes

The Company's authorized capital consists of:
- **Class A Common Stock:** Founder shares with enhanced voting rights
- **Class B Common Stock:** Employee option pool shares  
- **Class C Common Stock:** Public/crowdfunding shares
- **Preferred Stock:** Institutional investor shares with liquidation preferences

## 2. SHARE TRANSFER RESTRICTIONS

### 2.1 General Restrictions

No shareholder may Transfer any shares without:
1. Compliance with applicable securities laws
2. Written consent of the Company (not to be unreasonably withheld)
3. Compliance with right of first refusal provisions
4. For Founders: Compliance with vesting schedules

### 2.2 Permitted Transfers

The following Transfers are permitted without Company consent:
1. **Family Transfers:** To spouse, children, or family trusts
2. **Estate Transfers:** By will, intestacy, or court order
3. **Entity Transfers:** To entities controlled by the shareholder
4. **Charitable Transfers:** To qualified charitable organizations

### 2.3 Vesting Schedules

**Founder Shares (Class A):**
- 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff
- 25% vests on first anniversary
- Remaining 75% vests monthly over 36 months
- Acceleration on involuntary termination or change of control

**Employee Shares (Class B):**
- 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff  
- 25% vests on first anniversary
- Remaining 75% vests monthly over 36 months
- Forfeiture of unvested shares upon termination

## 3. TAG-ALONG RIGHTS

### 3.1 Tag-Along Trigger

If any Major Investor proposes to Transfer shares to a third party, all other shareholders have the right to participate in the sale on the same terms and conditions.

### 3.2 Notice Requirements

The selling shareholder must provide 30 days written notice including:
- Identity of proposed purchaser
- Number of shares to be sold
- Purchase price and payment terms
- Other material terms and conditions

### 3.3 Participation Rights

Each shareholder may sell up to their pro rata portion of shares in the proposed transaction, calculated based on their percentage ownership of the Company.

### 3.4 Minimum Participation

Tag-along rights only apply to proposed sales of at least:
- 10% of outstanding shares, or
- $1,000,000 in aggregate value

---

## 4. DRAG-ALONG RIGHTS

### 4.1 Drag-Along Trigger

If shareholders representing a majority of outstanding shares (including preferred shares on an as-converted basis) approve a sale of the Company, they may require all other shareholders to participate.

### 4.2 Required Approvals

Drag-along rights may be exercised upon approval by:
- Majority of Common Stock (all classes voting together)
- Majority of Preferred Stock (voting separately)
- Board of Directors

### 4.3 Mandatory Participation

All shareholders must sell their shares on the same terms, subject to:
- Reasonable representations and warranties
- Proportionate allocation of transaction costs
- Escrow arrangements on same terms

### 4.4 Fair Value Protection

The purchase price must be at least:
- Fair market value as determined by independent appraisal, or
- Price per share equal to the highest price paid in any arms-length transaction within the prior 12 months

---

## 5. RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL

### 5.1 Company Right of First Refusal

Before any Transfer to a third party, the Company has the right to purchase the shares on the same terms and conditions offered by the third party.

### 5.2 Secondary Right of First Refusal

If the Company declines to exercise its right, the shares may be offered to existing shareholders pro rata based on their ownership percentage.

### 5.3 Exercise Period

- Company has 30 days to exercise right of first refusal
- Shareholders have additional 15 days after Company's decline
- Rights expire if not exercised within specified periods

### 5.4 Valuation for ROFR

For Transfers other than arm's length sales:
- Fair market value determined by independent appraiser
- Cost split equally between Company and transferring shareholder
- Binding 409A valuation may be used if less than 12 months old

## 6. INFORMATION RIGHTS

### 6.1 Financial Information

The Company shall provide to Major Investors:

**Monthly Reports:**
- Unaudited financial statements
- Cash flow statements  
- Key performance metrics
- Management dashboard

**Quarterly Reports:**
- Detailed financial analysis
- Budget vs. actual comparisons
- Customer and revenue metrics
- Market analysis and competitive position

**Annual Reports:**
- Audited financial statements
- Annual budget and business plan
- Management letter from auditors
- Cap table and option pool analysis

### 6.2 Strategic Information

Major Investors shall receive:
- Material contracts and agreements
- Board meeting minutes and resolutions
- Major hiring and termination decisions
- Intellectual property developments
- Regulatory or legal issues

### 6.3 Inspection Rights

Shareholders holding at least 5% may:
- Inspect corporate books and records (reasonable notice)
- Meet with management quarterly
- Attend board meetings as observers (non-voting)

## 7. BOARD COMPOSITION AND GOVERNANCE

### 7.1 Board Size and Composition

The Board shall consist of 5-7 members:
- 2 members designated by Founders
- 2 members designated by Preferred Shareholders (if any)
- 1 independent member (mutual agreement)
- CEO (if not a Founder)
- Additional members as approved by majority vote

### 7.2 Director Qualifications

Board members must have:
- Relevant industry experience or expertise
- No material conflicts of interest  
- Ability to dedicate sufficient time and attention
- Agreement to fiduciary duties and confidentiality

### 7.3 Board Committees

The Board may establish committees including:
- **Audit Committee:** Financial oversight and compliance
- **Compensation Committee:** Executive and employee compensation
- **Nominating Committee:** Director nominations and governance

### 7.4 Protective Provisions

The following actions require approval of Preferred Shareholders (if any):
- Amendments to Articles or Bylaws affecting Preferred rights
- Issuance of new securities (except under employee plans)
- Sale of all or substantially all assets
- Liquidation, dissolution, or winding up
- Changes to the size or composition of the Board

## 8. VOTING AGREEMENTS

### 8.1 Founder Voting

Founders agree to vote their Class A shares:
- For director nominees designated by Preferred Shareholders
- For employee stock option plans (up to 20% of outstanding shares)
- Against any transaction that would circumvent drag-along rights

### 8.2 Investor Voting

Major Investors agree to vote their shares:
- For qualified independent director candidates
- For reasonable employee compensation and benefit plans
- For amendments that protect all shareholder classes fairly

### 8.3 Voting Trusts

Shareholders may establish voting trusts or agreements to:
- Pool voting power for specific purposes
- Ensure representation on the Board
- Coordinate on major corporate decisions

## 9. ANTI-DILUTION PROTECTION

### 9.1 Preferred Stock Anti-Dilution

Series Preferred Stock has weighted average anti-dilution protection against dilutive issuances, with standard exceptions for:
- Employee option pool increases (up to 20%)
- Stock splits and dividends
- Conversion of convertible securities
- Strategic partner issuances (with Board approval)

### 9.2 Common Stock Preemptive Rights

Major Common shareholders have preemptive rights to maintain their ownership percentage in:
- New equity financings
- Convertible debt issuances
- Option pool expansions

### 9.3 Participation Rights

Major Investors have the right to participate in future financing rounds up to their pro rata ownership percentage.

---

## 10. LIQUIDATION AND DISTRIBUTION

### 10.1 Liquidation Waterfall

Upon liquidation, dissolution, or winding up, proceeds shall be distributed:

1. **Preferred Stock Liquidation Preference**
   - Series Preferred: 1x non-participating preference
   - Return of original investment before Common Stock

2. **Common Stock Distribution**
   - Remaining proceeds distributed pro rata among all Common Stock
   - Class A, B, and C share equally on per-share basis

### 10.2 Deemed Liquidation Events

The following shall be treated as liquidation events:
- Sale of all or substantially all Company assets
- Merger where shareholders receive less than 50% of surviving entity
- Exclusive licensing of all Company intellectual property

### 10.3 Distribution in Kind

The Company may distribute assets in kind, valued at fair market value as determined by independent appraisal.

## 11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

### 11.1 Mediation

Disputes shall first be submitted to binding mediation with a qualified mediator selected by mutual agreement.

### 11.2 Arbitration

If mediation fails, disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration under Delaware Arbitration Rules.

### 11.3 Jurisdiction and Venue

All legal proceedings shall be conducted in Delaware state courts, and all parties consent to personal jurisdiction.
