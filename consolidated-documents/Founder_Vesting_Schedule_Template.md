## 2. Acceleration Provisions

### 2.1 Single Trigger Acceleration
Upon any of the following events, [PERCENTAGE]% of unvested shares shall immediately vest:
- Involuntary termination without cause
- Constructive termination (material breach by Company)
- Death or permanent disability

### 2.2 Double Trigger Acceleration
Upon a Change of Control Event (as defined below) AND involuntary termination within 12 months:
- **100%** of unvested shares shall immediately vest

### 2.3 Change of Control Definition
"Change of Control Event" means:
- Sale of substantially all Company assets
- Merger where Company shareholders own less than 50% of surviving entity
- Sale of more than 50% of voting control to third parties

## 3. Repurchase Rights

### 3.1 Company Right of Repurchase
The Company has the right to repurchase unvested shares upon:
- Voluntary termination
- Termination for cause
- Breach of confidentiality or non-compete agreements

### 3.2 Repurchase Price
Unvested shares may be repurchased at the lower of:
- Original purchase price
- Fair market value at time of repurchase

### 3.3 Exercise Period
Company must exercise repurchase rights within 90 days of triggering event.

## 4. Forfeiture Conditions

### 4.1 Immediate Forfeiture
All unvested shares are immediately forfeited upon:
- Termination for cause
- Conviction of felony
- Breach of fiduciary duty
- Competition with Company during employment

### 4.2 Post-Employment Restrictions
Founder agrees to:
- 12-month non-compete period
- 24-month non-solicitation of employees/customers
- Perpetual confidentiality obligations

## 5. Tax Considerations

### 5.1 83(b) Election
Founder may file an 83(b) election within 30 days to:
- Pay taxes on current fair market value
- Avoid future ordinary income treatment
- Convert future gains to capital gains treatment

### 5.2 Early Exercise Option
Founder may exercise unvested options to:
- File 83(b) election on unvested shares
- Start capital gains holding period
- Subject to Company repurchase rights

## 6. Transfer Restrictions

### 6.1 Prohibited Transfers
Founder may not transfer vested shares without Company consent, except:
- Transfers to family members or trusts
- Transfers required by court order
- Transfers to Company

### 6.2 Right of First Refusal
Company has right of first refusal on any proposed third-party sale at same terms.

### 6.3 Tag-Along Rights
Other founders have tag-along rights on any approved third-party sale.

## 7. Documentation Requirements

### 7.1 Vesting Tracking
Company shall maintain records showing:
- Monthly vesting calculations
- Acceleration events
- Share transfers and exercises

### 7.2 Stock Certificates
Vested shares shall be evidenced by:
- Stock certificates marked with transfer restrictions
- Updated capitalization table
- Secretary's certificate of good standing

## 8. Dispute Resolution

### 8.1 Arbitration
All disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration under [ARBITRATION RULES].

### 8.2 Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of [STATE].

## 9. Amendment and Termination

### 9.1 Amendment
This Agreement may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.

### 9.2 Survival
Confidentiality and non-compete provisions survive termination of this Agreement.

## Alternative Vesting Structures:

### Early Stage Startup (Higher Risk):
- 25% at 6 months, then monthly for 42 months
- 50% acceleration on single trigger

### Established Company (Lower Risk):
- Standard 25% at 12 months, monthly thereafter
- No acceleration provisions

### Performance-Based Vesting:
- Milestone-based vesting tied to company metrics
- Revenue targets, user growth, funding rounds

## Common Modifications:
- Varying cliff periods (6-18 months)
- Different acceleration percentages
- Performance milestones
- Extended post-termination exercise periods
