- **Month 3:** Performance review, feedback session
- **Month 6:** Comprehensive review, career development planning

## Legal & Compliance

### Employment Law Compliance

#### At-Will Employment
Employment is at-will, meaning either party can terminate the relationship at any time with or without cause, except where limited by law or contract.

#### Wage & Hour Compliance
- **Overtime:** Non-exempt employees receive 1.5x pay for hours over 40/week
- **Meal Breaks:** 30-minute unpaid break for shifts over 6 hours
- **Rest Breaks:** 10-minute paid break for every 4 hours worked
- **Time Tracking:** Required for all non-exempt employees

#### Leave Policies
- **FMLA:** Eligible employees receive up to 12 weeks unpaid leave
- **State Leave:** Compliance with state-specific leave requirements  
- **Military Leave:** USERRA compliance for military service
- **Jury Duty:** Paid leave for jury service

### Workplace Policies

#### Anti-Harassment & Discrimination
ActiveLog maintains a zero-tolerance policy for harassment and discrimination. All employees are required to complete annual training and report any incidents immediately.

#### Complaint Procedures
1. **Direct Resolution:** Attempt to resolve with involved party if comfortable
2. **Manager Report:** Report to direct manager or skip-level if needed
3. **HR Report:** Contact HR at hr@activelog.com or anonymous hotline
4. **Investigation:** Prompt, thorough, and confidential investigation
5. **Resolution:** Appropriate corrective action and follow-up

#### Code of Conduct
All employees must:
- Act with integrity and honesty in all business dealings
- Respect confidentiality and intellectual property rights
- Avoid conflicts of interest or appearance thereof
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations
- Report violations of company policy or legal requirements

### Data Privacy & Security

#### Privacy Compliance
- **GDPR:** Full compliance for European customers and employees
- **CCPA:** California Consumer Privacy Act compliance
- **SOC 2:** Annual Type II audit and certification
- **Employee Training:** Annual privacy and security training required

#### Incident Response
- **Reporting:** All security incidents must be reported within 1 hour
- **Investigation:** Security team investigates all potential breaches
- **Notification:** Customer and regulatory notification as required
- **Documentation:** All incidents documented for audit and improvement
