## ARTICLE IV - AUTHORIZED CAPITAL STOCK

### Section 4.1 - Authorized Shares

The total number of shares of stock that the Corporation shall have authority to issue is **15,000,000** shares, consisting of:

- **10,000,000** shares of Common Stock, par value $0.001 per share ("**Common Stock**")  
- **5,000,000** shares of Preferred Stock, par value $0.001 per share ("**Preferred Stock**")

### Section 4.2 - Common Stock Rights

Each share of Common Stock shall entitle the holder to:
- One (1) vote on all matters submitted to shareholders
- Participate pro rata in dividends when declared by the Board
- Participate pro rata in distribution of assets upon liquidation (after Preferred Stock)
- No preemptive rights unless specifically granted

### Section 4.3 - Preferred Stock Designation

The Board of Directors is hereby authorized to:
- Designate series of Preferred Stock and fix their rights, preferences, and limitations
- Determine dividend rates, liquidation preferences, conversion rights
- Set voting rights, redemption terms, and other provisions
- File certificates of designation for each series

### Section 4.4 - Share Classes (As Initially Designated)

**Class A Common Stock** (Founder Shares):
- 2,000,000 shares reserved for founders and early employees
- Enhanced voting rights (10 votes per share on certain matters)
- Transfer restrictions and right of first refusal
- Vesting schedules as determined by Board

**Class B Common Stock** (Employee/Option Pool):
- 1,000,000 shares reserved for employee stock option plan
- Standard voting rights (1 vote per share)
- Subject to vesting schedules and forfeiture provisions

**Class C Common Stock** (Public/Crowdfunding):
- 7,000,000 shares available for public investment
- Standard voting rights (1 vote per share)
- Freely transferable (subject to securities law restrictions)
- Eligible for dividend distributions

**Series Seed Preferred Stock**:
- 5,000,000 shares authorized for institutional investors
- Liquidation preference: 1x non-participating
- Dividend preference: 8% cumulative
- Anti-dilution protection: Weighted average broad-based
- Board representation and protective provisions

## ARTICLE V - BOARD OF DIRECTORS

### Section 5.1 - Number and Election

The number of directors shall be fixed by the bylaws but shall not be less than three (3) nor more than nine (9). Directors need not be shareholders.

### Section 5.2 - Classification

The Board of Directors may be classified into up to three (3) classes with staggered terms as provided in the bylaws.

### Section 5.3 - Powers

The Board of Directors shall have the power to:
- Manage the business and affairs of the Corporation
- Adopt, amend, and repeal bylaws
- Declare dividends and authorize distributions
- Issue shares and determine consideration
- Approve major corporate transactions

## ARTICLE VI - LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

### Section 6.1 - Director Limitation

A director of the Corporation shall not be personally liable to the Corporation or its shareholders for monetary damages for breach of fiduciary duty as a director, except for liability for:

1. Any breach of the director's duty of loyalty to the Corporation or its shareholders
2. Acts or omissions not in good faith or involving intentional misconduct
3. Unlawful payments of dividends or stock repurchases (Section 174 of Delaware General Corporation Law)
4. Any transaction from which the director derived improper personal benefit

### Section 6.2 - Amendment Limitation

Any amendment, modification, or repeal of this Article VI shall not adversely affect any right or protection existing at the time of such amendment with respect to acts or omissions occurring prior to such amendment.

## ARTICLE VII - INDEMNIFICATION

The Corporation shall indemnify its directors, officers, employees, and agents to the fullest extent permitted by Delaware law, as such law may be amended from time to time.

## ARTICLE VIII - CORPORATE OPPORTUNITIES

### Section 8.1 - Renunciation

The Corporation renounces any interest or expectancy in corporate opportunities that may be presented to:
- Any director who is not an employee of the Corporation
- Any shareholder holding more than 5% of outstanding stock
- Any affiliate of the above parties

### Section 8.2 - Competition

Directors and shareholders may engage in business ventures and investments that may compete with the Corporation, subject to their fiduciary duties.

## ARTICLE IX - EXCLUSIVE JURISDICTION

Unless the Corporation consents to the selection of an alternative forum, the Delaware Court of Chancery shall be the sole and exclusive forum for:

1. Derivative actions brought on behalf of the Corporation
2. Actions asserting claims of breach of fiduciary duty
3. Actions asserting claims under Delaware corporate law
4. Actions to interpret, apply, enforce, or determine validity of the Articles or Bylaws

## ARTICLE X - AMENDMENT

These Articles of Incorporation may be amended in the manner prescribed by Delaware law and the bylaws of the Corporation.
