## BRIDGE PASSAGE FOR 4A ENDING REVISION

**Current 4A ending should be slightly modified to include**:

```markdown
As they walked toward home, Luna's mind was already working on new questions. 
"Skipper," she said, slowing her pace with the deliberate attention that meant 
important thoughts were forming. "When you were telling that story... I noticed 
you changed things based on how we were listening. Like when Dr. Chen looked 
worried during the battle part, you made it less scary. And when my attention 
started wandering during the philosophy, you added more action."

Dr. Chen looked surprised. "You caught that? I didn't even realize he was 
reading our responses."

"That's what made it feel like a conversation instead of just listening," 
Luna continued. "Like the story was paying attention to us while we were 
paying attention to it. How do you learn to do that?"

Skipper's tail stirred with the satisfaction of a teacher whose student had 
noticed something important. "That, little one, is a much longer conversation. 
The kind that might require another morning in the park."

"With more examples?" Luna asked hopefully.

"With different kinds of weather," Skipper replied mysteriously. "Stories 
navigate differently in different conditions."
```

## OPENING OF 4B CONNECTS SEAMLESSLY

**Three weeks later, Luna returns with**:
- Specific request to learn storytelling navigation
- Dr. Chen brings colleague facing communication crisis
- Skipper recognizes this as teaching opportunity with multiple audience conditions

**Opening lines of 4B**:
```markdown
"You said stories navigate differently in different conditions," Luna announced 
as she bounded toward the old oak tree, three weeks of curiosity condensed 
into determined purpose. "I want to learn how to read the weather."

Skipper lifted his head with the patient attention of a teacher whose student 
had returned with exactly the right question. Behind Luna, Dr. Chen approached 
with another woman whose healer's hands carried the particular tension that 
came from facing conversations where words could help or harm in equal measure.

"Perfect timing," Skipper murmured, reading the morning's unusual conditions. 
"It looks like we'll have more than one type of weather to practice with today."
```
