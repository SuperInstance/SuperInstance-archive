## BREAK-EVEN ANALYSIS

### Revenue Break-Even (Operating)

**Fixed Costs** (Year 3 monthly): ~$3M/month
- Salaries: $2M
- Infrastructure: $500K
- Other: $500K

**Variable Margin**: 88%

**Break-Even Revenue** = Fixed Costs / Variable Margin
= $3M / 0.88 = **$3.4M/month** = **$41M/year**

**Achieved**: Q3 Year 2 (per base case)

### Cash Flow Break-Even

**Monthly Burn** (Year 1): $326K average
**Series A Funding**: $15M
**Runway**: 46 months (without revenue growth)

**With Revenue Growth**:
- Cash flow positive: Month 24
- Cumulative cash consumption: $8.5M
- Remaining buffer: $6.5M

### Unit Economics Break-Even

**Per-Customer Break-Even**:
- Need LTV > CAC (obviously)
- Current: LTV $525 vs. CAC $35 = 15x (massive cushion)
- Break-even if LTV drops to $35 (unlikely - would require 95% churn)
