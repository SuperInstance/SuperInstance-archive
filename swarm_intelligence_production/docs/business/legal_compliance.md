## 12. INCIDENT RESPONSE

### 12.1 Data Breach Response Plan

**Immediate (0-24 hours)**:
1. Contain breach
2. Assess scope
3. Notify leadership
4. Engage legal counsel

**Short-term (1-3 days)**:
1. Investigate root cause
2. Remediate vulnerabilities
3. Draft notifications
4. Notify regulators (GDPR: 72 hours)

**Long-term (1-4 weeks)**:
1. Notify affected users
2. Offer credit monitoring (if applicable)
3. Public communication (if warranted)
4. Post-mortem and improvement

---

### 12.2 Legal Holds

**Trigger Events**:
- Litigation filed or threatened
- Regulatory investigation
- Government request

**Process**:
1. Identify custodians and data sources
2. Issue preservation notices
3. Suspend deletion policies
4. Document compliance
