## PRICING PSYCHOLOGY & OPTIMIZATION

### Anchoring Strategy
- Enterprise tier creates high anchor point
- Makes Pro tier feel like incredible value
- Hobbyist tier is "obvious choice" vs. Free limitations

### Value Metric Alignment
- Pricing based on value delivered (agent-hours), not cost to serve
- Transparent, predictable pricing
- Easy mental math ($9/month = $0.30/day)

### Upgrade Path Design
```
Free → Feel limitation, see what's possible
  ↓
Hobbyist → Accomplish real projects, want more speed
  ↓
Pro → Build serious applications, need collaboration
  ↓
Team → Scale team usage, need enterprise features
  ↓
Enterprise → Custom needs, security, compliance
```

### Churn Prevention
- Annual plans: 20% discount (2 months free)
- Downgrade protection: Keep data for 60 days
- Usage alerts prevent surprise bills
- Success team proactive outreach at risk signals
