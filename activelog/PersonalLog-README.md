# PersonalLog - Simple Digital Diary

A clean, simple diary app for your daily thoughts and memories.

## What is PersonalLog?

PersonalLog is a minimalist digital diary that lets you capture your thoughts quickly and easily. No clutter, no complexity - just you and your words.

## How to Use

1. **Write** - Type your diary entry in the text area
2. **Save** - Click the "Save Entry" button  
3. **View** - Your entries appear below, newest first

That's it! Your entries are automatically saved with date and time.

## Current Limitations

⚠️ **This is a beta version with basic features only:**

- No user accounts or login system yet
- Entries are stored locally in your browser
- No search or filtering options
- No export/backup features
- No mobile app (web only)
- No sharing or collaboration
