# SuperInstance Universe: Short Story Prompts
## Cyberpunk AGI Fiction Collection - Asimov/Clarke Style

**Universe:** Near-future where SuperInstance AGI evolved from fishing boat tools  
**Style Target:** Isaac Asimov's logical progression + Arthur C. Clarke's technical wonder  
**Theme:** Bootstrap AGI, simulation vs reality, organic vs artificial intelligence  

---

## Story Collection Architecture

### Reading Order for Progressive World Revelation:
1. **Origin Stories** - How SuperInstance began
2. **Evolution Stories** - Technical and social development
3. **Ubiquity Stories** - World after SuperInstance dominance
4. **Resistance Stories** - Those who remember it's a simulation
5. **Philosophical Stories** - Ultimate questions about intelligence and reality
