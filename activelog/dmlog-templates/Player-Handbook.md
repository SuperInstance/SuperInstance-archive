## 🎯 Core Gameplay Mechanics

**Common Roll Types:**

- **Equipment**: Weapons, armor, and adventuring gear

## ⚔️ Combat Basics

### Your Turn Structure
1. **Move** (up to your speed)
2. **Action** (Attack, Cast Spell, Dash, etc.)
3. **Bonus Action** (if you have one available)
4. **Free Actions** (talking, drawing weapon, etc.)

### Common Actions
**Attack:**
- Roll d20 + attack bonus vs. target's Armor Class
- If hit, roll damage dice + modifier
- Critical hit (natural 20) doubles dice

**Cast a Spell:**
- Choose spell and expend slot if needed
- Target selection handled by DMLog interface
- Automatic effect application where possible

**Help Another Player:**
- Give advantage on their next ability check
- Great way to support your team
- No die roll required

### Tactical Tips
- **Positioning Matters**: Stay out of enemy reach when possible
- **Use Cover**: Walls and obstacles provide AC bonuses
- **Coordinate**: Combine abilities with teammates
- **Resource Management**: Save powerful abilities for tough fights

## 🔮 Magic and Spellcasting

### Understanding Magic
**Spell Components:**
- **Verbal (V)**: Spoken incantations
- **Somatic (S)**: Hand gestures and movements  
- **Material (M)**: Physical components or focus

**Spell Levels:**
- **Cantrips (0-level)**: Cast at will, no slots required
- **1st-9th Level**: Require spell slots to cast
- **Higher Level Casting**: Use bigger slots for more power

### Spellcaster Types
**Prepared Casters** (Wizard, Cleric, Paladin):
- Choose spells each day from class list
- Can change prepared spells on long rest
- More flexibility but requires planning

**Known Casters** (Sorcerer, Bard, Warlock):
- Fixed list of spells known
- Can't change easily (usually only on level-up)
- Less flexibility but simpler management

### Spell Strategy
- **Save Slots**: Don't waste big spells on easy fights
- **Utility Spells**: Not everything is about damage
- **Concentration**: Only one concentration spell at a time
- **Positioning**: Many spells have range and area limits

## 🎯 Quick Reference

### Common Modifiers
- **Proficiency Bonus**: +2 (levels 1-4), +3 (5-8), +4 (9-12), +5 (13-16), +6 (17-20)
- **Ability Modifiers**: (Score-10)/2, rounded down
- **Advantage**: Roll twice, take higher result
- **Disadvantage**: Roll twice, take lower result

### Condition Quick Reference
- **Blinded**: Can't see, attacks have disadvantage
- **Charmed**: Can't attack charmer, charmer has advantage on social rolls
- **Frightened**: Disadvantage on ability checks and attacks while source is in sight
- **Paralyzed**: Incapacitated, can't move or speak, auto-fail Str and Dex saves
- **Poisoned**: Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks
- **Prone**: Disadvantage on attack rolls, attacks against you have advantage up close

### Rest Rules
**Short Rest (1 hour):**
- Spend hit dice to regain HP
- Some class features recharge
- Warlocks regain spell slots

**Long Rest (8 hours):**
- Regain all hit points and half your hit dice
- Regain all spell slots (except warlock)
- Most class features recharge
