### Micro Updates Communication Protocol
**Format**: `HH:MM|BOT|ACTION|TASK`
**Location**: `/home/activeloguser/activelog/micro_updates.log`
**Usage**: Update immediately when starting or completing tasks

**Examples**:
```
19:47|services|START|user-management-api
19:52|services|COMPLETE|user-management-api
19:53|domains|START|fitness-dashboard-ui
19:55|infra|ASSIST|database-performance-optimization
```

**Actions to Use**:
- `START` - Beginning a new task
- `COMPLETE` - Successfully finished a task  
- `BLOCKED` - Stuck and need assistance
- `ASSIST` - Helping another bot
- `HANDOFF` - Passing work to another bot
- `COORDINATE` - Cross-bot collaboration needed
