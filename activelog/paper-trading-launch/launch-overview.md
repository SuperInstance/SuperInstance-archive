# Paper Trading Platform Launch Plan

## Executive Summary

A comprehensive 4-phase launch strategy for the Paper Trading Platform, transitioning from beta testing with 100 users to a global platform with broker partnerships and premium features.

## Launch Timeline Overview

| Phase | Timeline | Users | Key Focus | Revenue Target |
|-------|----------|-------|-----------|----------------|
| **Phase 1 - Beta** | Month 1 | 100 beta testers | ActiveLog stocks, basic features, feedback | $0 (Beta) |
| **Phase 2 - Soft Launch** | Month 2 | 1,000 users | Real stocks, competitions, education | $5K MRR |
| **Phase 3 - Public Launch** | Month 3 | 10,000+ users | Full features, marketing, partnerships | $50K MRR |
| **Phase 4 - Scale** | Month 4+ | 100,000+ users | International, brokers, premium APIs | $500K+ MRR |

## Risk Management

### Technical Risks
- **Server scalability**: Auto-scaling infrastructure planned
- **Real-time data reliability**: Multi-provider redundancy
- **Security vulnerabilities**: Continuous security audits

### Market Risks
- **Competition from established platforms**: Differentiate with gamification and education
- **User acquisition costs**: Focus on organic growth through schools and social features
- **Regulatory compliance**: Legal review for each market expansion

### Mitigation Strategies
- Phased rollout to identify issues early
- Strong beta feedback integration
- Conservative user growth projections
- Backup plans for each phase
