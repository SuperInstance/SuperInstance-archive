### [DOCUMENT 3] BACONS_LAW_FRAMEWORK.md  
**Status**: ANALYZED
**Priority**: HIGH - Mathematical constraints and social network theory

#### Core Concepts Identified:
1. **Bacon's Law for Neural Networks**
   - Any two neurons connected through ≤6 intermediate neurons
   - Frequently communicating neurons migrate to require fewer separation degrees
   - Named after "Six Degrees of Kevin Bacon" for public accessibility
   - Mathematical constraint: d_neural(n_i, n_j) ≤ 6

   - Bacon_Tensor ∈ ℝ^6 with dimensions: [frequency, specialization, priority, connection_density, usage_pattern, proximity_preference]
