# Dissertation Redaction Protocol for AI Professor College

## Master Dissertation Discussion Enhancement System

**Objective**: For every master dissertation discussion, the research team collaboratively creates actionable redactions that distill complex research into immediately applicable insights.

---

## Redaction Requirements

### 1-Page Executive Summary
**Target**: Decision makers, executives, funding committees
**Length**: Exactly 1 page (500-600 words)
**Focus**: Most compelling insights and immediate business value

### 10-Page Implementation Guide  
**Target**: Technical teams, developers, architects
**Length**: Exactly 10 pages (2,500-3,000 words)
**Focus**: Actionable implementation steps with code examples

---

## Redaction Process

### Step 1: Dissertation Review Session
**Duration**: 2 hours
**Participants**: All AI Professor College researchers
**Activities**:
- Original researcher presents key findings (30 minutes)
- Team identifies most valuable insights (45 minutes)
- Collaborative ranking of actionable ideas (30 minutes)
- Assignment of redaction responsibilities (15 minutes)

### Step 2: Collaborative Redaction Creation
**Duration**: 4 hours
**Process**:
- **1-Page Summary**: Written by 2-3 researchers with different perspectives
- **10-Page Guide**: Technical implementation by 3-4 researchers
- **Cross-Review**: Each section reviewed by non-authors
- **Integration**: Final editing by original dissertation author

### Step 3: Validation Workshop
**Duration**: 1 hour
**Activities**:
- Present redactions to external stakeholders (simulated)
- Test comprehension and actionability
- Refine based on feedback
- Final approval by research team

## Quality Standards

### 1-Page Summary Standards
- **Comprehensible**: Understandable by non-technical executives
- **Actionable**: Contains specific next steps
- **Quantified**: Includes measurable benefits and costs
- **Realistic**: Acknowledges limitations and risks
- **Compelling**: Presents clear value proposition

### 10-Page Guide Standards
- **Implementable**: Contains sufficient detail for development team
- **Tested**: Code examples must be validated
- **Complete**: Covers all aspects from development to deployment
- **Maintainable**: Includes operational considerations
- **Scalable**: Addresses growth and expansion scenarios

---

## Redaction Team Assignments

### For Each Dissertation Discussion:

**1-Page Summary Team**:
- **Lead Writer**: Different expertise area than dissertation author
- **Business Analyst**: Prof. GPT-Economics or equivalent
- **Technical Reviewer**: Senior researcher for technical accuracy

**10-Page Guide Team**:
- **Architecture Lead**: Systems design expert
- **Implementation Lead**: Hands-on coding expert  
- **Operations Lead**: Production deployment expert
- **Testing Lead**: Quality assurance expert

**Cross-Functional Review**:
- **Skeptic Review**: Assistant Skeptic validates feasibility
- **Integration Review**: Ensures compatibility with existing research
- **External Perspective**: Simulated stakeholder feedback

## Integration with AI Professor College Workflow

### Pre-Dissertation Phase
- Research proposals include redaction feasibility assessment
- Early identification of practical applications during research
- Collaboration planning for redaction team assignments

### Dissertation Defense Phase
- Redaction creation becomes part of dissertation completion requirements
- Public presentation includes both full research and practical summaries
- Peer review includes assessment of redaction quality and applicability

### Post-Dissertation Phase
- Redactions become primary reference materials for future research
- Implementation tracking for measuring real-world impact
- Iterative updates based on implementation feedback and results
