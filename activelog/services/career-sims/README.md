# 🚢 Maritime Career Simulations

Professional development games focused on maritime and logistics industries. Experience realistic career progression from entry-level positions to industry leadership through hands-on simulation.

## 🎯 Game Overview

Build your maritime career through four distinct professional paths, each with authentic industry mechanics, progressive difficulty, and real-world learning opportunities.

### Available Career Paths

#### 🎣 **Commercial Fishing Career**
**Path:** Deckhand → Able Seaman → First Mate → Captain → Fleet Manager → Fleet Owner

**Key Mechanics:**
- Seasonal fishing cycles and migration patterns
- Weather dependency and safety management  
- Government quotas and sustainable fishing
- Market volatility and price fluctuations
- Equipment maintenance and vessel operations

**Skills:** Seamanship, Fishing Techniques, Navigation, Leadership, Business Acumen

#### 🏖️ **Marine Tourism Career** 
**Path:** Tour Guide → Dive Instructor → Charter Captain → Resort Manager → Tourism Director → Tourism Mogul

**Key Mechanics:**
- Seasonal demand patterns
- Customer satisfaction and reviews
- Competition analysis and pricing
- Weather impact on operations
- Reputation-based premium pricing

**Skills:** Hospitality, Diving, Marketing, Communication, Strategic Planning

#### 🔧 **Shipyard Management Career**
**Path:** Apprentice → Marine Welder → Production Supervisor → Project Manager → Shipyard Manager → Owner

**Key Mechanics:**
- Project complexity and timeline management
- Material cost fluctuations  
- Quality standards and regulatory compliance
- Deadline pressure and penalty systems
- Technology integration and efficiency

**Skills:** Welding, Engineering, Project Management, Quality Control, Operations

#### 📦 **Freight Logistics Career**
**Path:** Dock Worker → Crane Operator → Logistics Coordinator → Port Manager → Regional Director → Global Executive

**Key Mechanics:**
- Global trade volume impacts
- Fuel cost management
- Port congestion and delays
- Automation adoption curves
- International regulations and tariffs

**Skills:** Logistics, Machinery Operation, Global Trade, Strategic Planning, Network Optimization
