# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

**Effective Date:** [DATE]

**GDPR Article 28 Compliant**

## 1. Parties and Definitions

### 1.1 Parties
**Controller:** The customer or entity using ActiveLog services ("Customer," "you," "your")  
**Processor:** ActiveLog Inc., a Delaware corporation ("ActiveLog," "we," "our," "us")

**Address:** [COMPANY ADDRESS]  
**Contact:** dpo@activelog.com

### 1.2 Definitions
For the purposes of this DPA:

**"Affiliate"** means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party

**"Controller"** means the entity that determines the purposes and means of processing Personal Data

**"Data Subject"** means an identified or identifiable natural person

**"GDPR"** means Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council

**"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person

**"Processing"** means any operation performed on Personal Data

**"Processor"** means the entity that processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller

**"Sub-processor"** means any third party engaged by the Processor to process Personal Data

**"Supervisory Authority"** means an independent public authority established by an EU Member State

## 2. Scope and Application

### 2.1 Agreement Scope
This DPA governs the processing of Personal Data by ActiveLog on behalf of the Customer in connection with the provision of ActiveLog Services ("Services").

### 2.2 Legal Framework
This DPA is designed to comply with:
- EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and CPRA
- Other applicable data protection laws

### 2.3 Precedence
In case of conflict between this DPA and the main service agreement, this DPA takes precedence regarding data protection matters.

## 4. Controller and Processor Obligations

### 4.1 Customer Obligations (Controller)
The Customer warrants and undertakes that:

**Legal Compliance:**
- Has a lawful basis for processing Personal Data
- Complies with all applicable data protection laws
- Has obtained necessary consents from Data Subjects
- Provides required privacy notices to Data Subjects

**Instructions and Configuration:**
- Processing instructions are lawful and documented
- Service configuration reflects privacy requirements
- Data retention periods are set appropriately
- Access controls and permissions are properly managed

**Data Subject Rights:**
- Responds to Data Subject requests within legal timeframes
- Implements appropriate technical and organizational measures
- Conducts Data Protection Impact Assessments when required
- Notifies ActiveLog of any relevant changes to processing purposes

### 4.2 ActiveLog Obligations (Processor)
ActiveLog warrants and undertakes that:

**Processing Compliance:**
- Processes Personal Data only on documented instructions
- Ensures confidentiality of Personal Data
- Implements appropriate security measures
- Assists with Data Subject rights requests

**Incident Response:**
- Notifies Customer of Personal Data breaches without undue delay
- Provides assistance with breach notification obligations
- Implements measures to address security incidents
- Documents all incidents and response measures

**Audits and Compliance:**
- Maintains records of processing activities
- Provides information necessary for compliance audits
- Allows for and contributes to audits by Customer
- Demonstrates compliance with data protection obligations

## 6. International Data Transfers

### 6.1 Transfer Safeguards
When Personal Data is transferred outside the EEA/UK, ActiveLog ensures appropriate safeguards through:

**Adequacy Decisions:**
- Transfers to countries with adequacy decisions
- Regular monitoring of adequacy status
- Alternative safeguards if adequacy is withdrawn

**Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs):**
- EU Commission-approved SCCs for controller-processor transfers
- UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA)
- Additional safeguards for high-risk transfers

**Supplementary Measures:**
- Technical measures: encryption, pseudonymization
- Organizational measures: access controls, audits
- Contractual measures: enhanced processor obligations
- Regular assessment of transfer conditions

### 6.2 Transfer Impact Assessments
ActiveLog conducts Transfer Impact Assessments (TIAs) for:
- Transfers to countries without adequacy decisions
- High-risk processing activities
- Government access risk assessments
- Regular review and updates

**Documentation:**

## 8. Data Subject Rights

### 8.1 Assistance with Rights Requests
ActiveLog will assist Customer in responding to Data Subject rights requests, including:

**Right of Access:** Providing information about processing and copies of Personal Data
**Right to Rectification:** Correcting inaccurate or incomplete Personal Data
**Right to Erasure:** Deleting Personal Data when legally required
**Right to Restrict Processing:** Limiting processing in certain circumstances
**Right to Data Portability:** Providing data in structured, machine-readable format
**Right to Object:** Stopping processing based on legitimate interests
**Rights Related to Automated Decision-Making:** Providing human review options

### 8.2 Response Procedures
- Customer is primarily responsible for responding to Data Subject requests
- ActiveLog provides technical assistance and data access as needed
- Response timeframe: within 5 business days of Customer request
- Documentation of all requests and responses maintained
- Coordination with Customer's legal and privacy teams

## 9. Data Breach Notification

### 9.1 Notification Timeline
In case of a Personal Data breach, ActiveLog will:
- Detect and contain the breach immediately
- Assess the scope and impact within 4 hours
- Notify Customer within 24 hours of becoming aware
- Provide written notification within 72 hours
- Provide ongoing updates as investigation progresses

### 9.2 Notification Content
Breach notifications will include:
- Nature and scope of the breach
- Categories and approximate number of affected Data Subjects
- Categories and approximate number of affected Personal Data records
- Likely consequences of the breach
- Measures taken or proposed to address the breach
- Measures to mitigate possible adverse effects

### 9.3 Customer Obligations
Customer acknowledges responsibility for:
- Assessing whether notification to Supervisory Authorities is required
- Notifying Supervisory Authorities within 72 hours if required
- Notifying affected Data Subjects if required
- Documenting the breach and response measures

## 10. Audits and Compliance

### 10.1 Audit Rights
Customer has the right to:
- Conduct audits of ActiveLog's processing activities
- Request information necessary to demonstrate compliance
- Engage qualified third-party auditors
- Receive copies of relevant compliance certifications

### 10.2 Audit Procedures
- Customer provides reasonable advance notice (30 days)
- Audits conducted during business hours with minimal disruption
- ActiveLog may charge reasonable costs for extensive audits
- Confidentiality obligations apply to audit findings
- Remediation plans developed for any identified issues

### 10.3 Compliance Documentation
ActiveLog maintains and provides access to:
- SOC 2 Type II audit reports
- ISO 27001 certification documents
- Penetration testing results
- Security assessment reports
- Data processing records

## 11. Return and Deletion of Data

### 11.1 Data Return Options
Upon termination or expiration of Services, Customer may request:
- Return of all Personal Data in commonly used format
- Secure deletion of all Personal Data
- Continuation of processing for limited transition period
- Transfer to alternative processor

### 11.2 Deletion Timeline
- Customer data export available for 90 days after termination
- Personal Data deleted within 30 days of termination (unless legally required to retain)
- Backup systems purged within 90 days
- Written confirmation of deletion provided to Customer

### 11.3 Legal Hold Exceptions
Personal Data may be retained longer if:
- Required by applicable law
- Subject to legal proceedings or investigations
- Needed for defense of legal claims
- Customer provides written instructions to retain

## 12. Liability and Indemnification

### 12.1 Limitation of Liability
ActiveLog's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitation of liability clauses in the main service agreement.

### 12.2 Data Protection Specific Liability
Each party is liable for:
- Damages caused by processing in violation of GDPR
- Failure to meet their respective obligations under this DPA
- Unauthorized or unlawful processing of Personal Data
- Processing contrary to lawful instructions

### 12.3 Indemnification
ActiveLog will indemnify Customer against:
- Fines imposed due to ActiveLog's non-compliance with this DPA
- Claims arising from ActiveLog's breach of data protection obligations
- Costs incurred due to ActiveLog's security incidents

## 13. Term and Termination

### 13.1 Term
This DPA remains in effect for the duration of the main service agreement and any processing of Personal Data thereafter.

### 13.2 Survival
The following provisions survive termination:
- Data return and deletion obligations
- Confidentiality obligations
- Liability and indemnification provisions
- Audit rights for 3 years post-termination

### 13.3 Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate this DPA if:
- The other party materially breaches data protection obligations
- Continued processing would violate applicable law
- Required by Supervisory Authority order
