## 1. Introduction

ActiveLog Inc. ("ActiveLog," "we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Services, including our website, applications, and platform features.

This Policy applies to all users of ActiveLog services worldwide and has been designed to comply with:
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) - European Union
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) - California, USA
- Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) - USA
- Other applicable privacy laws and regulations

**By using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.**

## 6. Data Security and Protection

### 6.1 Security Measures

**Technical Safeguards:**
- End-to-end encryption for sensitive data
- AES-256 encryption for data at rest
- TLS 1.3 for data in transit
- Multi-factor authentication options
- Regular security audits and penetration testing

**Organizational Measures:**
- Employee privacy and security training
- Access controls and principle of least privilege
- Incident response and breach notification procedures
- Regular security policy updates
- Third-party security assessments

**Infrastructure Security:**
- SOC 2 Type II compliant hosting
- DDoS protection and monitoring
- Network segregation and firewalls
- Regular backup and disaster recovery testing
- 24/7 security monitoring

### 6.2 Data Breach Response

In the event of a data breach, we will:
- Contain and investigate the incident within 24 hours
- Notify affected users within 72 hours (or as required by law)
- Provide clear information about the nature and scope of the breach
- Offer guidance on protective measures users can take
- Implement additional safeguards to prevent recurrence

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

### 7.1 Retention Periods

**Account Information:** Retained while your account is active and for up to 2 years after deletion for legal and security purposes

**Content and Projects:** 
- Active repositories: Retained indefinitely while account is active
- Deleted repositories: Soft-deleted for 90 days, then permanently removed
- Private content: Deleted immediately upon user request

**Usage and Analytics Data:** Aggregated and anonymized data may be retained indefinitely for service improvement

**Communication Records:** Support tickets and communications retained for 3 years

**Legal Hold:** Data may be retained longer when required by legal obligations or ongoing investigations

### 7.2 Data Deletion Rights

You have the right to:
- Delete your account and associated data
- Request specific data deletion (where legally permitted)
- Export your data before deletion
- Receive confirmation of data deletion
