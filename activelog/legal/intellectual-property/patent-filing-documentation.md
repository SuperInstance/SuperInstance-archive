## 1. Patent Strategy Overview

### 1.1 Strategic Objectives
ActiveLog's patent strategy is designed to:
- Protect key innovations and technical differentiators
- Build defensive patent portfolio against litigation
- Create licensing opportunities and revenue streams
- Enhance company valuation and competitive positioning
- Support business development and partnership negotiations

- Include system and method claims

- Competitive alternatives
- Commercial applications
