# Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP)

**[COMPANY NAME], Inc.**  
**2024 Equity Incentive Plan**

**Effective Date:** [DATE]

## 1. Plan Administration

### 1.1 Purpose
This Plan is intended to:
- Attract and retain qualified employees
- Align employee interests with company growth
- Provide long-term incentive compensation
- Support company's strategic objectives

### 1.2 Administration
The Plan is administered by the Board of Directors or designated Compensation Committee.

### 1.3 Share Reserve
The total number of shares reserved for issuance under this Plan is **[NUMBER] shares** of Common Stock.

## 2. Eligibility and Types of Awards

### 2.1 Eligible Participants
- Full-time employees
- Part-time employees (at Board discretion)
- Consultants and advisors (if permitted by law)
- Officers and directors

### 2.2 Types of Equity Awards
- Incentive Stock Options (ISOs)
- Non-Qualified Stock Options (NQSOs)
- Restricted Stock Awards (RSAs)
- Restricted Stock Units (RSUs)
- Stock Appreciation Rights (SARs)

## 3. Stock Option Terms

### 3.1 Exercise Price
- ISOs: Not less than fair market value on grant date
- NQSOs: As determined by Board (typically fair market value)

### 3.2 Vesting Schedule (Standard)
- **4-year vesting period**
- **1-year cliff** (25% vests after 12 months)
- **Monthly vesting** thereafter (1/48th per month)

### 3.3 Exercise Period
- Options expire **10 years** from grant date
- Earlier expiration upon termination (see Section 4)

### 3.4 Exercise Methods
- Cash payment
- Cashless exercise (if approved)
- Stock swap (using already-owned shares)
- Net exercise (for NQSOs)

## 4. Termination Provisions

### 4.1 Voluntary Resignation
- **90 days** to exercise vested options
- All unvested options are forfeited

### 4.2 Termination for Cause
- All options (vested and unvested) are immediately forfeited
- No post-termination exercise period

### 4.3 Involuntary Termination (Without Cause)
- **90 days** to exercise vested options
- Potential acceleration per individual agreements

### 4.4 Death or Disability
- **12 months** for heirs/estate to exercise vested options
- Potential acceleration per individual agreements

### 4.5 Retirement
- **90 days** to exercise vested options
- Board may extend exercise period

## 5. Change of Control Provisions

### 5.1 Definition of Change of Control
- Acquisition of 50% or more voting control
- Sale of substantially all company assets
- Merger where shareholders receive less than 50% of surviving entity

### 5.2 Treatment of Options
Options may be:
- **Assumed** by acquiring company
- **Substituted** with equivalent awards
- **Cashed out** at fair value
- **Accelerated** (partial or full vesting)

### 5.3 Double Trigger Acceleration
For key employees, 100% acceleration upon:
- Change of control AND
- Involuntary termination within 12 months

## 6. Tax Considerations

### 6.1 Incentive Stock Options (ISOs)
- **Qualification Requirements:**
  - Exercise price ≥ fair market value
  - Maximum 10-year term
  - $100,000 annual limit on exercisable ISOs
  - Employee must hold stock 2 years from grant, 1 year from exercise

- **Tax Treatment:**
  - No regular tax on exercise
  - Potential Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT)
  - Capital gains treatment if qualification requirements met

### 6.2 Non-Qualified Stock Options (NQSOs)
- **Tax Treatment:**
  - Ordinary income on exercise (spread between FMV and exercise price)
  - Capital gains treatment on subsequent sale
  - Company tax deduction equal to employee's ordinary income

### 6.3 Early Exercise and 83(b) Elections
- Employees may exercise unvested options
- 83(b) election must be filed within 30 days
- Starts capital gains holding period on unvested shares

## 7. Option Grant Guidelines

### 7.1 Typical Grant Sizes by Role

| Role Level | Equity Range | Typical Vesting |
|------------|--------------|-----------------|
| C-Level | 0.5% - 3.0% | 4yr/1yr cliff |
| VP Level | 0.1% - 1.0% | 4yr/1yr cliff |
| Director | 0.05% - 0.5% | 4yr/1yr cliff |
| Senior IC | 0.01% - 0.25% | 4yr/1yr cliff |
| Mid-Level | 0.005% - 0.1% | 4yr/1yr cliff |
| Junior | 0.001% - 0.05% | 4yr/1yr cliff |

### 7.2 Refresh Grants
- Annual refresh grants based on performance
- Typically 25-50% of initial grant size
- New 4-year vesting schedule

### 7.3 Promotion Grants
- Additional grants upon promotion
- Bridge gap to new role's standard grant size

## 8. Valuation and Fair Market Value

### 8.1 Private Company Valuation
Fair market value determined by:
- Board resolution based on 409A valuation
- Independent third-party appraisal
- Recent arm's length transaction values

### 8.2 409A Valuations
- Required for private companies
- Must be updated annually or upon material events
- Safe harbor protection if followed

### 9.2 Anti-Dilution Adjustments
