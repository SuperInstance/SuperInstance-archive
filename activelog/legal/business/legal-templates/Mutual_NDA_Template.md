# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

**Effective Date:** [DATE]

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between:

**Party A:** [COMPANY NAME], a [STATE] [ENTITY TYPE]  
**Address:** [ADDRESS]

**Party B:** [COMPANY NAME], a [STATE] [ENTITY TYPE]  
**Address:** [ADDRESS]

## 1. Purpose

The parties wish to explore a potential business relationship and may disclose confidential information to each other ("Purpose"). This Agreement governs the disclosure and use of such confidential information.

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

### 2.1 Confidential Information Includes:
- Technical data, trade secrets, know-how, research, inventions
- Business plans, financial information, customer lists
- Marketing strategies, pricing information, cost data
- Software, source code, algorithms, databases
- Employee information, organizational charts
- Any information marked "Confidential" or that would reasonably be considered confidential

### 2.2 Confidential Information Excludes:
Information that:
- Is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement
- Was known to receiving party before disclosure
- Is independently developed without use of confidential information
- Is rightfully received from a third party without breach
- Is required to be disclosed by law or court order (with notice to disclosing party)

## 3. Obligations and Restrictions

### 3.1 Non-Disclosure
Each party agrees to:
- Hold all confidential information in strict confidence
- Not disclose confidential information to any third party
- Use confidential information solely for the Purpose
- Protect confidential information with same care used for own confidential information (but no less than reasonable care)

### 3.2 Limited Access
Each party shall:
- Limit access to confidential information to employees and advisors with a need to know
- Ensure all recipients are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as this Agreement
- Remain liable for any breach by its employees or advisors

### 3.3 Non-Use
Neither party shall use the other's confidential information to:
- Compete with the disclosing party
- Develop competing products or services
- Solicit disclosing party's employees or customers
- Reverse engineer or attempt to derive source code or trade secrets

## 4. Term and Survival

### 4.1 Term
This Agreement shall remain in effect for **[NUMBER] years** from the Effective Date, unless terminated earlier by either party with **30 days** written notice.

### 4.2 Survival of Obligations
Confidentiality obligations shall survive termination and continue for **[NUMBER] years** after termination, or indefinitely for trade secrets.

## 5. Return or Destruction of Information

Upon termination of this Agreement or upon request, each party shall:
- Return all confidential information in any form
- Delete all electronic copies and derivatives
- Provide written certification of compliance
- Exception: May retain one copy for legal compliance purposes only

## 6. Remedies

### 6.1 Equitable Relief
Each party acknowledges that breach would cause irreparable harm, and agrees that:
- Monetary damages would be inadequate
- Disclosing party is entitled to injunctive relief
- Such relief may be sought without posting bond
- Equitable relief is in addition to other available remedies

### 6.2 Disclosure Required by Law
If compelled to disclose by law or court order, receiving party shall:
- Promptly notify disclosing party
- Cooperate in seeking protective order
- Limit disclosure to minimum required
- Mark disclosed information as confidential

## 7. No Obligation to Disclose

Nothing in this Agreement obligates either party to:
- Disclose any particular confidential information
- Enter into any business relationship
- Refrain from developing competing products independently

## 8. No License or Transfer

No license or transfer of intellectual property rights is granted by disclosure of confidential information. All rights remain with the disclosing party.

## 9. General Provisions

### 9.1 Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of **[STATE]**, without regard to conflict of law principles.

### 9.2 Jurisdiction and Venue
Any disputes shall be resolved in the courts of **[STATE/COUNTY]**.

### 9.3 Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement regarding confidential information and supersedes all prior agreements on this subject.

### 9.4 Amendment
This Agreement may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.

### 9.5 Severability
If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder shall remain in full force and effect.

### 9.6 No Waiver
Failure to enforce any provision does not constitute a waiver of that provision or any other provision.

### 9.7 Counterparts and Electronic Signatures
This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original.

## Best Practices for NDA Management:

### Before Signing:
- [ ] Define the purpose clearly
- [ ] Limit scope to necessary information
- [ ] Set reasonable time limits
- [ ] Consider reciprocal vs. one-way structure

### During Term:
- [ ] Mark confidential materials clearly
- [ ] Track what information is shared
- [ ] Limit access on need-to-know basis
- [ ] Regular compliance monitoring

### Upon Termination:
- [ ] Request return/destruction of materials
- [ ] Obtain certificates of compliance
- [ ] Update information inventory
- [ ] Continue monitoring for compliance

## Common Modifications:

### Standard Terms:
- **Duration:** 2-5 years for most business discussions
- **Survival:** 3-5 years for confidentiality obligations
- **Notice Period:** 30 days for termination

### High-Stakes Deals:
- **Duration:** Indefinite for trade secrets
- **Enhanced Security:** Specific handling requirements
- **Liquidated Damages:** Predetermined damage amounts
- **Audit Rights:** Right to verify compliance
